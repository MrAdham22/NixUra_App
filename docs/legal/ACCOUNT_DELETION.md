# Deleting your NixUra account and data

This page is the **web-accessible deletion path** for Google Play's account-deletion requirement. Publish it at a public URL
(for example on the NixUra site, nixura.web.app) and enter that URL in Play Console > App content > Data safety > Account deletion.

## In the app (fastest)
Settings > Account > **Delete account**, confirm (accounts with a password enter it again). This erases straight away:
your synced data in our database (habits, events, good-habit logs, profile and settings), your Notion link if you made one,
and your account itself (email, sign-in methods). The app then signs you out.

## If you cannot open the app
Email **adhamelgendydev@gmail.com** from the address of the account with the subject "Delete my NixUra account". We will
confirm it is you by replying to that address and delete the account and its data within 30 days (normally within a few days).

## What is kept
- Bug reports you sent while signed in are kept until the problem is handled. Ask in the same email if you want them deleted.
- Counts of AI coach messages per day are deleted automatically after 3 days.
- Anonymous usage stats are not linked to your account; Firebase deletes them after 2 months.
- We keep no backups of synced data.

## What stays on your device
Habits and events stored on the phone stay there until you use Settings > Erase all data or uninstall the app.
